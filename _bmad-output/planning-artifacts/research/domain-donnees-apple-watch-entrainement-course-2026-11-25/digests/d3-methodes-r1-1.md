## Synthesis for the planner (provisional, built from the claims above)

- **Default TID for recreational runners: pyramidal.** Mostly easy running, a moderate amount of threshold/tempo, a little VO2max work. Claims 1, 2, 4 and 8 support this. Avoid threshold-dominant plans as the default (claim 6). Polarized is a sound alternative for more competitive or higher-volume runners (claims 2 and 3). Overall the choice between them matters little (claims 1 and 3), so the one-line explanation should say so rather than overclaim.
- **Hard safety rule, stronger evidence than the TID choice:** no single run may exceed 110% of the longest run of the last 30 days (claim 20). Trust low-detail input less, and ask clarifying questions (claims 21-22).
- **Taper:** 2 weeks (up to 3 for the marathon), volume reduced 41-60%, intensity and frequency kept (claims 18-19).
- **Method labels** (Daniels, 80/20, Norwegian, MAF, run-walk) are coaching systems. Only run-walk (one RCT) and TID in general have trial evidence. MAF's formula is unvalidated (claim 15). The Norwegian model is elite, lactate-guided, and untested in amateurs (claims 12-13).

## (b) Leads worth chasing

1. Full text of Rosenblat 2025: the recreational-subgroup effect sizes and which studies drive them. This is key for the "for whom" rule.
2. Stöggl & Sperlich 2014 (Front Physiol, polarized vs HIIT/threshold/high-volume) and the Seiler 2010 TID canon. Not retrieved this round.
3. Petreça 2025 (IJSSC) and the multi-model AI marathon study (ResearchGate 401029168): error types for current LLMs, beyond GPT-3.5.
4. Full text of Hottenrott 2016 (exact run-walk ratio) and any run-walk RCT for beginners or 5k.
5. Pfitzinger (*Advanced Marathoning*), Hansons (*Hansons Marathon Method*: 16-mile long-run cap, "cumulative fatigue", 6 days/week), and Lydiard. Primary descriptions were not retrieved; the Hansons site URL returned 404.
6. Periodization in runners (linear vs block vs reverse), e.g. the Filipas et al. 2022 16-week pyramidal→polarized RCT in well-trained runners (PMC9299127, seen in results, not read).
7. Haugen et al. 2022 world-class runner review (PMC8975965), for long-run proportion guidance.
8. Individual response variability (responders/non-responders) in TID trials. Not searched.
9. Garmin-RUNSAFE follow-ups on long-run caps per distance.
